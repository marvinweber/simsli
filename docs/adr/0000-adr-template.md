## Context

The problem and the forces constraining the solution: requirements,
constraints, prior decisions that apply here. Facts, not arguments —
the reasoning shows up in the next section.

## Decision

The choice, in one or two sentences, active voice ("We will …").
Briefly note the alternatives considered and why they lost.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, which trade-offs are
consciously accepted. Include the negatives — an ADR without honest
downsides is marketing, not a record.
