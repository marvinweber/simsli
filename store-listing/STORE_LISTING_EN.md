## Full Description

```text
Simsli is a modern, simple, and shared shopping list designed specifically for households who want grocery shopping to be quick, organized, and stress-free.

🏪 Filter by Store — Only See What You Need
Never scroll past hardware store items while standing in the supermarket aisle. Assign items to one or multiple stores (e.g. Supermarket, Discount, Bakery, Drugstore) and filter your list with a single tap. See only what you actually need where you are.

👥 Shared in Realtime for Households
Plan your shopping together. Household members can add items, check off entries, and update quantities simultaneously. Changes sync instantly across all devices.

⚡ Fast & Frictionless Adding
Add items in seconds with instant search and smart catalog suggestions. Specify quantities, units (e.g., kg, liter, pieces), and personal notes with a tap.

🧺 Intelligent Catalog & Smart Lifecycle
• Permanent items: Common groceries like milk, bread, and coffee remain in your catalog for quick one-tap re-adding.
• One-time items: Need birthday candles or party supplies? Mark them as one-time items and they disappear once completed.
• Automatic cleanup: Checked-off items stay in "Recently checked" for quick undo and clean up automatically after 24 hours — no manual clutter.

📶 Offline-First by Design
Poor mobile reception inside the supermarket? Simsli works completely offline. Your changes are saved locally immediately and sync automatically as soon as you are reconnected.

✨ Clean & Modern Material Design
Built with modern Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive, Simsli offers a responsive, accessible, and delightful dark/light theme experience.

Key Features:
• Store-based list filtering (single or multiple stores per item)
• Realtime synchronization across all household devices
• Simple household invites via code
• Categories with emojis for aisle-by-aisle shopping
• Quantities, units, and item comments
• 100% offline functionality
• Respectful privacy — no ad trackers, no bloated permissions
```
